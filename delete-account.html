# Delete Account Request - SportsPal This page provides clear instructions for users who wish to delete their SportsPal account and associated data. ## 🧾 How to Request Account Deletion To request the deletion of your SportsPal account, please send an email to: 📩 **[sportspalapplication@gmail.com](mailto:sportspalapplication@gmail.com)** Make sure your email includes: * Your username or email linked to the account * A clear request to delete your account and data ## ⏱️ Processing Time We process deletion requests within **7 business days**. You will receive a confirmation once your account and data have been deleted. ## 🔐 Data Handling Upon deletion: * Your account information, chat history, and game participation data will be **permanently removed**. * Certain information (e.g., legal logs or abuse reports) may be **retained temporarily** as required by law. All data is encrypted in transit and securely stored until deletion is confirmed. ## 🛡️ Privacy First We take privacy seriously. Learn more in our full Privacy Policy: 👉 [https://babushkaboy1.github.io/sportspal-privacy/](https://babushkaboy1.github.io/sportspal-privacy/) If you have questions or concerns, don’t hesitate to reach out. Thank you for using SportsPal!
