Request to Delete Your SportsPal Account

If you'd like to permanently delete your SportsPal account and all associated personal data, please send an email request to us at:

Email: sportspalapplication@gmail.com

What to Include

Please include the following details to help us verify and process your request:

  • Your SportsPal username or email used in the app
  • Optional: Reason for deletion (to help us improve)

What Happens After

Once we verify your request, we will permanently delete your account and associated data within 7 days. You will receive an email confirmation.

Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data beyond the account deletion process. Some anonymized app activity logs may be retained for internal analytics purposes only, in compliance with data protection laws.
