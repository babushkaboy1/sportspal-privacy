Privacy Policy for SportsPal

Effective Date: May 3, 2025

Thank you for using SportsPal. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Username, profile picture, city and neighborhood selection, and email address (if provided through third-party logins such as Google, Apple, or Facebook)
  • Location Data: Approximate and precise location to show nearby sports games and courts, GPS coordinates for court submissions or joining games
  • Device Data: Device ID and model for performance, crash reporting, and notifications
  • Activity Data: Games created, joined, rated, and courts submitted
  • In-app Messaging: Messages sent between users

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and personalize your experience
  • Match you with relevant sports games and players
  • Send notifications about games, updates, and ratings
  • Improve app performance and maintain a safe environment

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Sharing Your Information

We may share limited data with trusted third parties only in these cases:

  • With service providers to host and manage features (e.g., Firebase, Expo)
  • If required by law or legal process
  • To protect the rights and safety of SportsPal and its users

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security and limit access to your data. However, no system is 100% secure, and we encourage safe practices.

5. Children’s Privacy

SportsPal is not intended for users under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has used SportsPal, please contact us.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Update or delete your profile via the app
  • Request data deletion by emailing us at sportspalapplication@gmail.com
  • Disable location or notifications from device settings

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy over time. All changes will be posted in the app. Continued use of SportsPal signifies acceptance.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us at:

Email: sportspalapplication@gmail.com
