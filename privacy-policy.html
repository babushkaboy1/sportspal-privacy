Privacy Policy for SportsPal

Effective Date: May 3, 2025

Thank you for using SportsPal. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Username, profile picture, city and neighborhood, email address (if using third-party logins like Google, Apple, or Facebook).
  • Location Data: Approximate and precise location to suggest nearby courts and games, GPS for joining or adding courts.
  • Device Data: Device ID and model for performance logs and bug reports, push notification tokens.
  • Activity Data: Games created, joined, or rated; courts submitted; chat messages between users.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and personalize your experience on SportsPal
  • Match you with relevant sports games and players
  • Send reminders, ratings, and event updates
  • Improve features and maintain a safe environment

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Sharing Your Information

We may share limited data with trusted service providers to:

  • Host and manage services (e.g. Firebase, Expo)
  • Send notifications and analytics
  • Comply with legal requirements

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data. While no system is 100% secure, we limit access to your data and recommend safe usage habits.

5. Children’s Privacy

SportsPal is not intended for users under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used our app, contact us immediately to review or delete their data.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Update or delete your profile through the app
  • Disable location or notifications in your device settings
  • Request data deletion via email

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our features or legal requirements evolve. We’ll notify you of major updates through the app. Continued use signifies acceptance of any changes.
