**Delete Account Request for SportsPal**

We respect your privacy and are committed to providing a clear and easy way to request the deletion of your SportsPal account and any associated data.

---

### ✉️ How to Request Account Deletion:

If you wish to delete your account and all related data, please send an email to:

**[sportspalapplication@gmail.com](mailto:sportspalapplication@gmail.com)**

Make sure to include the following:

* Your username or the email you used in the app
* Subject line: "Delete My SportsPal Account"

We will process your request within **7 business days**.

---

### 📅 What Happens When You Request Deletion:

* Your account will be permanently deleted from our system.
* All your associated data (including profile, games, and activity history) will be erased.
* This action is irreversible.

---

### ⏳ Data Retention Policy:

For legal or administrative reasons, some minimal data (e.g., transaction logs) may be retained for up to **30 days** after deletion, after which they are automatically purged.

---

### 🔒 Security:

All user data is encrypted in transit. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

---

For more information, please refer to our [Privacy Policy](https://babushkaboy1.github.io/sportspal-privacy/).

Thank you for using SportsPal.
